## Notes
- **What did the AI get right?** Standard textbook definitions, high-level structural breakdowns, and standard conceptual explanations were consistently clear and grammatically fluent.
- **What did it get wrong or leave unsupported?** Boundary constraints (sailing vs. steamship), hardware implementation realities (analog sensor vs. tinyML), and physical safety margins (battery DoD and inverter loss).
- **What did I learn about verification?** Never treat grammatical fluency or conversational confidence as proof of technical accuracy. True verification requires inspecting silent assumptions against primary literature and physical constraints.
